Build the foundation.

Roadmap

Set the operating priorities

Develop a practical People roadmap with the CEO and CFO, sequencing policy, systems, and workforce needs.

Standards

Define core policies, performance expectations, and employee-relations escalation practices.

HR Support

Clarify HR support needs

Identify employee and manager support needs, clarify internal points of contact, and establish practical processes for HR questions, issue escalation, and follow-through.

RWEFIRST 90
DAYS

06 / Days 61–90

Days 61–90.
Prepare to scale.

Hiring

Build the workforce growth plan

Partner with business leaders and Finance to prioritize 30+ anticipated hires, assess hiring capacity, and establish a scalable recruiting model supported by a contract recruiter.

PEO Assessment

Evaluate PEO Services, Costs & Long-Term HR Needs

Evaluate TriNet’s current services, costs, benefits, and service performance to determine the value provided as the organization grows. Partner with Finance to assess the full financial, staffing, and operational implications of transitioning in-house, including benefits administration, FMLA, COBRA, multistate payroll, and HR coordination. Weigh potential savings against added costs, administrative responsibilities, and operational risks to determine the most sustainable approach as the business scales.

Accountability

Measure what matters

Establish reporting on hiring, workforce cost, retention, and transition risks for executive review.
